A practical guide

Questions to bring to every model output.

A thoughtful review starts with curiosity about what the result means and where it may fall short.

Are the inputs right?

Check that the information used for an assessment is accurate and relevant to the person being considered.

What is in scope?

Understand what the estimate is intended to describe and what it does not cover.

What context is missing?

Consider clinical details and patient preferences that may not be represented in the available data.

How will we follow up?

Use discussion and appropriate follow-up to put any estimate in its proper place.
