RAG field guide

Questions to ask of a document-based answer.

Good retrieval is only part of the workflow. Check document scope, source passages, and update history when an answer matters.

Is the right collection connected?

Make sure the relevant HR, finance, legal, or IT documents have been uploaded and indexed.

Which document supports this?

Open the cited source and verify the passage, version, and surrounding language.

Is there a newer policy?

Check whether an updated agreement or procedure supersedes the document returned in search.

Who owns the next step?

Use the answer to find the owner or process, then follow the organization's approval workflow.
