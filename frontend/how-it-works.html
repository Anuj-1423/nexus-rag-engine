From company documents to grounded answers.

Nexus RAG gives teams a conversational way to find information across shared policies, technical guides, agreements, and personal reference documents.

See the workflow

A clear path from upload to review.

01 / Add documents

Bring in the knowledge

Upload supported company files such as PDF, DOCX, TXT, or CSV. Shared documents can be managed centrally while people keep personal references in their own collection.

02 / Choose scope

Search the right collection

Select shared company knowledge, personal files, or both. This keeps a question focused on documents that are relevant to the person and task.

03 / Ask naturally

Find useful passages

Ask in everyday language. Nexus combines semantic and keyword retrieval, then reranks matching passages to assemble useful context for the response.

04 / Verify

Check the source

Review the answer alongside retrieved document context. For important decisions, confirm the exact wording and current version in the original file.

Knowledge stays organized by access scope.

Keep frequently used company guidance in a shared library, while personal collections support an individual's working documents. Nexus can search across either scope according to the selected mode.

Shared library

Administrators manage the common policies, handbooks, runbooks, and agreements available to authorized users.

Personal collection

Users can add their own reference material and search it privately as part of their workflow.

Hybrid retrieval

Semantic matches and keyword search work together to retrieve passages for a question.

Human review

Treat generated responses as a way into the source material. People remain responsible for interpretation and decisions.
