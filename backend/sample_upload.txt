This is a sample enterprise knowledge base document about employee onboarding, payroll, and leave policy. The company uses a hybrid RAG system for internal document search. Onboarding steps include HR registration, identity verification, equipment setup, and benefits enrollment. Payroll is processed on the 15th and 30th of each month and is based on the approved timesheet and salary structure. Employees can request leave through the HR portal and must provide a reason and start date. The system helps answer questions about payroll dates, onboarding process, and leave request procedures.
